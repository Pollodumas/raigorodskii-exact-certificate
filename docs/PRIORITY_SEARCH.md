# Bibliographic and priority search

## Status

No prior appearance of the explicit sextic or the exact rational root isolation was found in the sources searched. This is evidence, not proof of priority.

- the exact decimal prefix `1.2395667407265985397`;
- the complete coefficient tuple `144,-528,648,-220,-71,42,-31`;
- the monic integral tuple `1,-22,162,-330,-639,2268,-10044`;
- combinations of `Raigorodskii`, `minimal polynomial`, `algebraic`, `sextic`, `root isolation`, and `lower-bound constant`;
- OEIS searches for the decimal expansion and both coefficient tuples;

- accessible arXiv and bibliographic records for the cited lower-bound papers;
- accessible metadata and full texts for neighboring unit-distance algebraic certificates.

## Limitations

The search did not exhaust MathSciNet, zbMATH, Google Scholar, unindexed theses or source code, paywalled full texts, or Russian-language literature beyond accessible metadata and texts. Equivalent algebraic-number presentations may also evade coefficient searches. The manuscript therefore uses only a qualified `to the best of our knowledge` statement and makes no unconditional claim of priority.

The identification of the one-variable optimum with Raigorodskii's lower-bound base is inherited from Näslund's explicit statement. The arXiv v2 reciprocal display was checked. The rendering in the typeset Mathematika version remains unverified.
